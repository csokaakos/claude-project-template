# Decision Log

Tracks significant decisions made during this project — what was chosen, what the alternative was, and why.
Purpose: prevent re-arguing settled questions in future sessions.

---

## How to add an entry

Add an entry whenever:
- A non-obvious design or technical choice is made
- An alternative was explicitly considered and rejected
- A requirement changed from a previous position

Format:

### [Short decision title]
- **Decided:** what was chosen
- **Alternative considered:** what was rejected
- **Reason:** why
